## Quality Checklist

Before finalizing, verify:

- Laterality is preserved.
- Medication names, doses, and frequencies match the source.
- Dates and durations match the source.
- Negatives such as "no fever" or "denies chest pain" are preserved.
- Any contradiction is flagged instead of fixed silently.
