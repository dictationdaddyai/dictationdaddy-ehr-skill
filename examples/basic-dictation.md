# Example

Input:

```text
Patient is a 52-year-old male follow-up for hypertension, says home readings have been around 130 over 80, no chest pain, no shortness of breath, taking lisinopril 10 milligrams daily, continue current medication, check BMP, follow up in 3 months.
```

Output:

```text
Subjective

52-year-old male presenting for follow-up of hypertension. Home blood pressure readings have been approximately 130/80. He denies chest pain and shortness of breath. He is taking lisinopril 10 mg daily.

Objective

Not stated.

Assessment

Hypertension, controlled on current regimen.

Plan

- Continue lisinopril 10 mg daily.
- Check BMP.
- Follow up in 3 months.
```
